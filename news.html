*Breaking News*

Some concerns

  • There have been some questions raised over the fairness of a double funnel. This has been in relation to a specific scenario. That is what happens when two people cross the finish line at exactly the same time in different funnels. To answer this, let’s use a single funnel example first.

    When two runners cross the line together, one those may want to ‘beat’ the other by getting a lower valued token so they may push down the funnel ahead of the other person to ensure this. Or, both people just fall in line, without caring, or one will offer for the other to go ahead. This is common and we see it a lot of course especially during busy times. Ultimately, someone needs to come first out of the two.

    In a double funnel, the runner who would want to push through to get a lower valued token (remember, this isn’t about time) is denied that opportunity and so is out of control as to which one of the two gets ‘first’ place. parkrohn deals with this scenario by assigning one of the runners an earlier position than the other. Remember, the time for all athletes will still be correct and the token number received has no relation to the finish position so there really is no value in being concerned about this unless it’s a lower position number being sought.

    We understand the accolade of first place and so to avoid this scenario happening for the fastest of runners, the first x number of runners are ideally routed through a single funnel so even if two cross the line together they can establish between them their final positions.

    We think this is a fair solution and really only has a negative impact on runners seeking a lower token number than the person with the same time as them. Also, it’s fairer on another level and that is more clicks per second can be achieved by the timekeepers and so a more accurate time is likely if 2-3 cross the line together in each funnel. In a single funnel it’s quite hard for a timekeeper to press 6 times in a single second!

    It’s also worth noting the final position at a parkrun event is based purely on the order in which people cross the finish line and has no relation to when they crossed the start line. The reality is that someone coming a single place behind someone else may in fact be many places before or after the other runner depending on when each of them crossed the start line. It is not actually a true representation of one's position, but only a ‘marker’ so that timed results can be achieved.

NEWS

Observations since running a double funnel

Funnel duckers and funnel managing

Our initial fears with running a double funnel, was that funnel duckers could (in certain circumstances) be slightly more of a problem than with a single funnel, however, a double funnel actually appears to pretty much eliminate any funnel ducking whatsoever. We think the reason for this is twofold.

  • Funnel duckers tend to dodge the funnel because they don't want to queue, using a double funnel removes the queue and as such it's as fast for a runner to continue to the end as it is to try to break out at some point along the way. We've seen that the data we are getting is much more accurate and funnel managers report not actually having to do anything as it all runs so smoothly, we feel that we certainly need no additional funnel managers, in fact if anything the opposite is true.
  • Ducking between funnels. We've seen no evidence of this happening, probably because there is no value in doing so! If people want to duck out, they aren't going to duck out to anther funnel. We think our intial concerns over this were rather unfounded! The information about funnel duckers is still included in the user guide below for information.

Timekeepers

We have seen far more accurate data when using a double funnel, this is obviously because the timekeepers are under far less pressure than they would be with a single funnel. We are seeing the number of tokens to number of time results is better because of this and also because of the lack of funnel duckers.

Finish tokens

The pressure on the finish token volunteers has been reduced, they are no longer being overwhelmed and pressured to go as fast as possible.

Setting up the funnels

The funnel at Preston Park traditionally is a zigzag which whilst not difficult to set up, is more time consuming that setting up two straight shorter funnels.

Results processing

While there is always going to be additional processing required as the functionality isn't native to EMS, we've made it as simple as possible to process the results. We are now able to display names of athletes which helps, we are also seeing less requirement to 'tinker' with the results due to much better data as a result of the observations above. We would even go as far as to say that the additional steps to process the results is much less of a pain than dealing with multiple funnel duckers and other inaccuracies of data.
